(04) Case study

Tidewater Ferries

Making a 90-year-old ferry line feel like the best part of the trip.

  1. A

    The knot

    Three islands, four ticket systems and a logo nobody could draw from memory. Locals loved the boats. Tourists booked the plane.

  2. B

    The move

    We rebuilt the brand around the tide itself: a T that rises out of the water, a timetable-led app, and signage that reads from the deck of a moving boat.

  3. C

    The result

    One booking flow across every route, rolled out on 14 vessels and 9 piers in a single season.
